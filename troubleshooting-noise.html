Diagnose Grain, Blur, Flicker, and Color Shifts on Calls

A symptom-led troubleshooting guide for low-light webcam noise, motion blur, flicker, unstable exposure, and color.

Resolve grain with useful light first

Grain usually appears when the camera amplifies a weak signal. Add or move a soft face light, reduce a bright competing background, then reevaluate gain.
